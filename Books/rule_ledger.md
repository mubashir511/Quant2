extracted book text with `python -m tools.nlm.verify_quotes` (EXACT / squashed = PDF word-splits / NOT FOUND);
**data** = our own test on real broker data (`tools/studies`, deep M5 history where noted); **status** = adopted /
rejected / untested. Research answers (NotebookLM, unverified paraphrase with page citations) are in
